# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating whether to work with ColorMath: founders, product/engineering executives, and investors who need senior product design + engineering leadership. They arrive from a referral, a personal-site link, or LinkedIn, usually already half-sold on Craig or Jessica individually. Their job: confirm these two are credible together, understand what the studio does, and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

colormath.com is the landing page for ColorMath, a product design and engineering collaboration studio between two senior executives: Jessica Tenuta (cofounder/CPO of Packback, scaled to 3M+ students and a 2024 PE acquisition; Forbes 30 Under 30; Shark Tank alum) and Craig M Booth (CTO of Packback; former computational astrophysicist). Color = design, Math = engineering. Success is a visitor understanding the pairing in ten seconds and sending an email. Content is managed in Sanity CMS.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, playful. The confidence of two operators who have already built and exited, expressed with craft and a light touch rather than corporate polish. Copy is first-person-plural, short, and concrete.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing page: hero metrics, icon card grids, gradient CTAs, testimonial carousels.
- Stuffy corporate consultancy: McKinsey/Accenture tone, handshake stock photos, "leverage synergies" jargon.
- Trendy design-portfolio site: awwwards motion, cursor tricks, marquee text, spectacle for its own sake.

## Design Principles

1. **The pairing is the product.** Every section should reinforce design × engineering as one intertwined practice, not two service lines.
2. **Practice what you preach.** The page itself is the portfolio; craft in type, color, and geometry is the proof of competence.
3. **Ten-second clarity.** A visitor should be able to say what ColorMath is and who's behind it without scrolling past the first fold.
4. **Bold over polish.** Saturated color fields, serif conviction, human cutout photography. Confident and loud, never sterile.
5. **One ask.** The only conversion is an email conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Black-on-cream text everywhere for contrast; pastels are backgrounds and accents, never text-on-cream. Honor prefers-reduced-motion. Semantic landmarks and alt text throughout.
